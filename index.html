Frequently asked questions

How does ordering on WhatsApp work?

Add products to your bag, tap checkout and fill in your details. WhatsApp opens with your full order pre-written — just press send. We confirm the order and payment details with you there.

Which payment methods do you accept?

You choose Cash on Delivery or UPI/online payment at checkout; we confirm the details on WhatsApp.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive in 3–7 working days depending on your location. We share tracking on WhatsApp once shipped.

How long until I see results?

Skin renews over roughly 4–6 weeks. Acne usually improves in 6–8 weeks and pigmentation in 8–12 weeks with consistent use and daily sunscreen.

Should I patch test?

Yes — always patch test a new product on your inner forearm or behind the ear for 24 hours. If you have a skin condition or are pregnant/nursing, check with your doctor first.
